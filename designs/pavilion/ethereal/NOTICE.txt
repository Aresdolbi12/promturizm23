Adaptation for the Southern Russian Industrial Tourism Forum:
The panel sequence and content were changed for the forum. Its palette,
type, photographs, local assets, programme details and registration are new.
main.css retains the original layout, responsive panel and image rules.
Unused components, demo imagery, external font and icon imports were removed.
main.js retains Ethereal's scrolling and navigation framework, adapted for
form controls, accessible anchors, reduced motion and short screens. Edge
hover scrolling, the old wheel normalization helper and the demo lightbox
were omitted or disabled.
